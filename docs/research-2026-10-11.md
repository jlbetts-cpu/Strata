# Research, 2026-10-11

Four things the owner asked to have looked into. Each was read by an
independent researcher against the code and the web; sources are at the
end of each part. What is marked "not verified" was not.

## 1. Albums

**As built before today.** An album is not stored. It is a view of your
wins, made again each time Memories is drawn (`Album.monthShelf`): a title
you photographed three times on two days in the month, your wins sent to
one crew that month, or a past day ("a year ago today"). Up to five, one
row, under the month's photo count. A card was 108 points wide on a 402
point phone, while one photo in the grid under it is 132.7: an album was
smaller than a single photo inside it. Nothing could hide one.

**What others do.**

| App | Removing something it made for you | Bringing it back | Your own albums |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Photos | Hold: Delete Memory, or Feature Less (this day, place, person) | Settings, Reset Suggested Memories (all at once) | Yes; deleting an album keeps the photos |
| Google Photos | Remove this memory; hide this date; show a face less | Settings lists hidden dates and faces, each removable | Yes |
| Spotify | Exclude from your taste profile | Same menu, Include again | n/a |
| Day One | Per-journal "Show in On This Day" switch | Switch back | Journals |
| BeReal | Delete from Memories is permanent | None | No |

**Built today.** Hold an album: Remove Album (this one) or Never Show
(every album of that title, that crew, or past days). Undo for five
seconds. Settings, Memories, Removed Albums lists each with Show Again.
Wins and photos are never touched. Cards are 152 points: bigger than a
photo, 2.3 on screen so the third still peeks.

**Your own albums: later, and not hand-picked.** A hand-picked album needs
stored membership, a picker, a rename, a cover and empty states, and would
live on one phone unless it goes in the model, which needs the iCloud
schema deployed. Several days. The cheap honest version: in this app the
title is the album, so "Keep as Album" on a title would give that title a
card whatever its photo count. About half a day, when you want it.

Not verified: no source publishes tile sizes in points; Apple Music and
Spotify shelves show about 2.3 to 2.5 cards from memory.

## 2. Photo strip templates (research only, nothing built)

Two facts first. The strip has no composed 9:16 Story card today: it
exports a transparent PNG to the share sheet. And the app cannot tell where
a strip was shared: only saved, crew and "other" are ever recorded, so no
template could be judged until that is fixed.

| # | Template | Where it goes | Why it would spread | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story sticker: the strip as a movable sticker on a paper-colour ground | Instagram Story | Wrapped-style cards: 500M shares in a day (secondary source) | Needs a Meta app ID and a phone to test |
| 2 | Classic four-cut: four equal frames | Print 2x6, iMessage | Korean four-cut booths: 120M visits since 2017 | Layout only |
| 3 | Doubles: two strips on one 4x6, one to give away | Print, iMessage | Booths sell prints in pairs | One wrapper view |
| 4 | Week sheet: the week's strips side by side | Feed, Story | Retro, weekly and friends-only: about 7M downloads | New composition |
| 5 | Single instant: one photo, deep foot | Feed, iMessage | Instax past 100M units | New preset |
| 6 | 2x2 grid | Feed | Opinion only | Grid mode |
| 7 | Front and back pair | Carousel | Opinion only | Export the back |
| 8 | Month contact sheet | Feed | Opinion only | Fights "less is more" |

**Order I would build:** record the real share destination first; then
four-cut; then doubles; then the Story sticker. **Not building:** a receipt
or stats card. It spreads (Receiptify passed a million uses), but it is a
list of counts with no photo, and here it would read as a score.

**Nearly free variation already in the code:** eight paper colours; four
print tightness presets, of which only one is wired; frame count up to
eight; pose. Not there: a caption line, a choice of date stamp.

**Bringing a friend back:** keep the foot wordmark the only mark and do
not enlarge it (Instagram recommends watermarked Reels less). Send the
give-away strip in iMessage with the crew invite link beside it. Not a QR:
a typical code gets 21 scans, and in a Story it sits on the phone you
would scan with.

## 3. Scanning a page of a paper journal (research only)

**Recommendation: later, after launch, and image only.**

Every serious app keeps the picture of the page; recognised text is a
bonus where it exists at all. Handwriting recognition is the weak point
everywhere: Day One's own staff say Scan Text "can sometimes" read
handwriting, Rocketbook asks for upright print, and the only number found
(about 82% on printed handwriting, 55% on cursive, method not described)
is anecdotal. A wrong transcript of a diary is worse than none.

**The version that fits this app, "ink lift":** photograph the page,
straighten it, and keep only the handwriting as black on clear. It then
draws in the app's warm ink like any of your drawings, follows dark mode,
and needs no white rectangle or shadow. It can live in the day's existing
sketch slot, so no model change and no backup change.

**Flow:** hold the journal's pen for Draw or Scan a Page; the app's own
camera with a quiet page outline; one still; Retake or Keep; the page
settles under the note.

**The animation:** the photo holds, the paper fades so only the
handwriting remains, the outline eases square, and the ink drifts down
into place under the words. It needs the app's own camera, not Apple's
scanner, which hands back an already-flat page and cannot be restyled.

**Risks:** ruled lines, creases and shadows becoming ink (prototype
first); a diary page sits unencrypted on the phone and in its backup, so
Journal Lock must cover it; the page does not reach a second phone; a day
holds a sketch or a page, not both.

**Time:** 4 to 5 days including a pass on a phone. With Apple's scanner
and no animation, 2 days. Turning it into typed text, 2 to 3 more.

Not verified: nothing was run on a phone; the thresholding is untested.

## 4. Your Three, the add sheet and the plan sheet, and colours

**Your Three: keep, and connect.** It is three wins you tend to log, kept
on this phone, ticked by a win of the same name today, shown atop today's
plan and first in the add sheet's row. Turning them into three repeating
plan lines would put three outlines on everyone's tower every morning and
fill the reminder, which is the debt the feature was built never to be. So
it stays its own thing, with four joins: not listed twice when a plan line
has the same words; not offered while planning; kept in backups; and
reachable from the plan.

**Add sheet against plan sheet.** They differ most in where you type (a
bold field at the top against a glass bar at the foot), what Return does,
which lines are listed, and the row of days ("Later" against "Today", no
dot, no scroll). Those are the ones a person feels.

**Colours into the block's menu.** Possible: the colour row comes off the
add sheet, the random colour stays the default, and the block's menu gets
a Colour list with the category drawings. It overturns three of your
earlier calls (the visible colour name after someone "was confused what
the color categories were for"; colour "optional, in Edit"; the plan and
edit sheets matching). And two costs: menu pictures draw in ink, not in
the colour, and on a photographed win colour becomes hold-only. This one
waits for your yes.
