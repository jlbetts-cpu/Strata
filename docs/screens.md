# Some Wins: every screen (updated 2026-10-15, after the night's build)

A map of the app as it ships, for brainstorming what to add next. Each
screen lists what is on it and what you can do there. Rare screens and
pop-ups are at the end.

**What the app is.** A daily photo journal for small wins, built for
people with ADHD. Every win is a block on today's tower. Blocks come in
three sizes: Quick, Regular and Deep. Photos land on a map and in
Memories. Friends share wins in small private crews. There is no account
and no server: everything lives on the phone and in the person's own
iCloud.

**Four tabs:** Wins, Crews, Camera, Memories. Memories is your profile;
Settings opens from its gear.

---

## 1. First run

**Launch.** The wordmark draws and erases itself, then hands off to the
tower.

**Onboarding.** Each page has a progress ring on glass, the same ring as
the app's goal crest.
- A short film of the tower being built.
- Your name. A name is required.
- Make your head: take a photo and it becomes a character.
- Your first win: draw a block out of the slot to choose its size.
- How quick wins work: hold the slot.
- A daily reminder time.
- Notifications, places, and photos permissions, asked in context.
- Lock Journal: an animated lock drawing, with Not Now.
- The first run ends on the tower with the slot ready.

**Make Your Head** (also reachable later). Camera, face detection,
cutout, and a choice of look. If no face is found, a box-head drawing is
shown.

## 2. Wins tab (the tower)

**The tower.** Today's wins are stacked as blocks on a lattice. Each
block shows its colour, a photo or doodle, and its size. Your head
stands on top as a companion.
- The empty slot: press it to Add, hold it to log a quick win, or draw a
  block out of it to choose its size.
- Press a block to open its menu: open the photo, Edit, Move, Replace,
  Remove photo, or Doodle.
- The goal crest: a head inside a ring that fills as the day fills. When
  the day's goal is reached, the head dances and a photo strip prints.
- Month picker: past days and months as smaller towers.
- An undo line after adding or deleting a win.

**Add a Win sheet.**
- Name, plus word suggestions where QuickType would be.
- Colour/category and size.
- A block you can press for Take Photo, Choose from Library, or Doodle.
- Note, mood and sketch, which go to the journal.
- Which crews to send it to, and who you were with.
- Delete, when editing a win.

**Plan.** Opens from the button on the Wins tab.
- Your Three: three lines pinned at the top. A press opens Add.
- Plan lines with days and weekday repeats. They show as glass outlines
  on the tower.
- Suggestions.
- A press on a planned line opens Take Photo, Choose from Library, Log
  Without a Photo, Edit, or Not Today.

**Day sheet / Your Day.** A summary of one day's wins.

**Block doodle sheet.** Draw on a block.

**Replays.** A weekly and monthly film of the tower building itself. You
can save the video or share it. A count-up header, simple dates.

## 3. Crews tab

**Crews list.**
- Today: a row of every friend's head across your crews; someone who has
  logged today is ringed in their newest win's colour and opens it; the
  rest are faded. Invite at the end.
- Your crews as cards: name, faces, and today's wins.
- New Crew.
- Join from a link: a walking-cat drawing while it joins, then a thumbs
  up when you're in.
- Signed out of iCloud: a cloud-in-sunglasses drawing.
- A first-visit empty state.

**New crew / invite.** Name the crew, get an invite link and code, and
share it.

**Crew tower.** The crew's shared tower for the day. Everyone's wins are
stacked, with their heads living in a little arena.
- Hold a win to get reactions (an emoji row, + for more, a sticker at
  the end) and Reply above them.
- Press a win to open the photo viewer: Do It Too (adds it to your
  plan), Hide for Me, Report, Remove Win from Crew. For your own wins,
  also Delete Win (new).
- The crew's slot: log a win straight into the crew.
- A crew plan: a checkbox block on the crew tower.
- Cards under the header, one at a time: Catch Up (the days you missed),
  the crew's week from Sunday evening, and a one-time widget tip.
- The chat drawer at the foot.

**Crew chat (a native sheet).**
- At rest: the newest line over the composer. A drag or a tap lifts it to
  reading; writing takes the whole height with the crew's name on top.
- A "New" marker where you left off; big emoji; links; reply starters on
  a friend's win; tap a reaction to see who; Hide for Me on a line.
- Messages, quoted replies to wins, and reactions on a hold (Reply,
  Copy, Report).
- The + menu: Win, Plan, Sticker, Strip.
- A picture viewer for chat photos.

**Crew day view.** A past day of the crew.

**Crew info sheet.**
- Members and their stats.
- Rename the crew.
- Hidden wins, with Show Again.
- Leave the crew, or Remove from Crew for a member (if you started the
  crew).
- Report, followed by a thanks card with a thumbs up.
- Crew rules and the age gate.

## 4. Camera tab

**Viewfinder.** The picture sits in a rounded card. A black band runs
above it. In light mode, the band below fades to black together with the
tab bar.
- Shutter: draw a block out of it to choose a size.
- Flip camera, flash, and zoom (a single pill).
- Film looks.

**Review.** Light in light mode, dark in dark mode.
- Crop box with a rounded, dimmed print outside it.
- Size, name, crews.
- Keep, which hands the photo to Add.

**Camera not allowed.** Still plain text. A drawing is still to come
from Jayden.

## 5. Memories tab

**Memories (your profile).**
- At the top: your head, your name, Score, Wins and Crews, and the streak
  as one line. A press on your head opens Edit Profile; on the figures,
  Your Score (how it adds up, the streak, the trend).
- Months, newest first, each with its month drawing. November's turkey
  runs at the end of the list.
- Photos for each month in a grid, split into Deep, Regular, and Quick.
- Albums carousel: hold an album to remove it.
- A house card: invite a friend, leave a tip, or Plus.

**Month / calendar view.** A month as a calendar of small towers.

**Day album detail.** One day's photos.

**Map.** Photos placed where they were taken, grouped by place. Tap a
place to see its photos.

**Photo viewer.** Light or dark at rest. A tap or a zoom turns it
immersive on black.
- A filmstrip.
- Share, Save to Photos, Edit, Remove Photo.
- Place name and time.

**Photo strip booth.**
- Square photos, booth style.
- Shake to develop.
- Doodle on the strip, then share it as a story.

---

## 6. Edit Profile and Settings

**Edit Profile.** Your picture, your name (which can't be emptied), your
heads and earlier heads. **Settings** is the gear on Memories.

**Settings.**
- Reminders: Daily Reminder, Reminder Time, Weekly and Monthly Replays,
  Past Wins.
- Completion Sounds and Haptic Feedback.
- Preview Your Week and Preview Your Month (replays).
- Save to Photos and Keep Places.
- Month Drawing and Removed Albums.
- Lock Journal: Face ID covers the journal. A thumbs up when it's
  switched on.
- How Some Wins Works.
- Back Up Everything (a zip), Restore From a Backup, and Reset All Data.
- Tip jar.
- Send Feedback and Rate on App Store.
- Privacy, Terms of Use, and Share Anonymous Usage.

**Restore.** Progress, then a thumbs up when it works, or the
scratching-cat drawing when it fails.

**Journal lock cover.** A lock over the journal until Face ID passes.

## 7. Rare screens and pop-ups

- **Store could not open:** the scratching-cat drawing.
- **Tip thank-you:** a thumbs up.
- **Feedback / review ask.**
- **All clear celebration:** when every planned win is done.
- **Goal reached:** the head dances and a photo strip prints.
- **Undo line:** after any add, delete, or send.
- **iCloud full:** a notice in Settings.
- **Notifications:** daily reminder, crew wins, chat, and replays ready.
  The social dot uses the Social coral. A friend's win offers 🔥, ❤️ and
  Reply from the alert; a chat line offers Reply.

## 8. Outside the app

- **Widgets:** "Today" (today's wins and their photos) and "Log a Win"
  (tap a size to add one).
- **Control Center:** a Log Win control.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** log a win, open a win, and a Focus filter.
- **Spotlight:** wins are indexed.
