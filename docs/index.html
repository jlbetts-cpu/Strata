The idea.

The good things you did today are the first ones you forget.

A win is anything you did

The walk. The call you made. The meal you cooked. There is no list of what you should have done, and nothing is ever marked missed.

It stacks

Each win is a block. A small one, or a big one if it took a lot out of you. By evening the day is a tower.

It stays

Add a photo and the day is kept. A month from now it is all still there, in the order you lived it.

How it works.

Log it

Tap the slot and name what you did. Or hold it for a quick one and name nothing at all.

Plan it

Write down what you mean to do. It waits on the tower as an empty box, and moving it to tomorrow is just planning.

Keep it

Reach your goal and the day's photos print as a strip. The month builds itself out of what you logged.

Designed with ADHD brains in mind.

With your people.

One tower for the day, with a few friends.

A crew is two to eight people. You see what they did. They see what you did. Nobody is ranked, and nothing counts against anyone.

Plan something together, and it shows up as a box on the crew's tower. Finish it and they know.

Crews

Small and private. A crew of two is a crew. Invite with a link, leave whenever you like.

Reactions

A heart, a crown, a sticker you cut out of your own photo. A reply when you have something to say.

Yours first

A win is on your own tower before it is anyone else's. You choose which ones a crew sees.

Win with your people.

Small wins still count. Start with one.
