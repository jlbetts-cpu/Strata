# The voice

The owner's, from his Launch Pack doc of 2026-10-12. Every string in the
app comes from one hand: notifications, tips, chat lines, empty states,
errors, buttons, recap and album text. **Hold this next to you while you
write any string.** If a line could appear in any other app, rewrite it
until it could only appear in this one.

## The core principle

Write spare by default, and let warmth arrive in single, specific beats.
Warmth is not adjectives, exclamation marks, or decoration. It is
understanding: writing like you get what this moment actually feels like
for the person. "You earned today" lands because it understands how it
feels to have a good day nobody noticed. One true human line against a
calm background is felt and remembered; a pile of warm lines is noise.

**The test for every line:** does it sound like a friend who gets it,
written by someone who cared about the words? Or like an app talking at
me? If it is the second, write it again.

## The foundation. Never break these.

- Spare. One idea per message. Short. Never make someone work to read it.
- Specific over generic. Ground every line in the real thing: the actual
  number, day or moment. "Your best Sunday in three weeks" beats "You're
  on a roll."
- Honest. Never inflate, never cheerlead, never praise for nothing.
- No exclamation marks. Ever.
- No em dashes. Periods or commas.
- No generic motivation. No "You've got this", no "Keep it up", no
  fortune cookies.
- No unprompted questions in notifications.
- One identity. Every message sounds like the same entity, a character
  and not a tone.

## Clarity is part of the voice

The app is new, so never assume the person already knows how it works.
When a gesture or idea is new, say the one true thing that unlocks it,
once, small, in context. Teach just in time: the tip appears the moment it
is relevant, never as a lecture up front, never all at once. One clear
hand on the shoulder, then out of the way.

## Warmth, the signature

- It is understanding, not decoration.
- It arrives in single beats, rarely: about one message in three. The
  restraint is what makes it land.
- It lives best in small, unexpected places: the empty state, the quiet
  day, the corner nobody expects to be spoken to in.

Without it: "Eight wins today. Your best Sunday in three weeks."
With it: "Eight wins today. Your best Sunday in three weeks. Good one to
look back on." That last clause is the whole signature.

## Notifications invite, they do not report

A notification's job is to earn the tap. If it states the whole thing on
the lock screen there is no reason to open the app. Tease outside,
specific inside: hold the number, the photo or the detail back for when
they open. The exception is a specific that is itself the hook ("Your best
Sunday in three weeks").

**Never notify someone about an absence.** Empty states and quiet days
live in the app only.

No decorative emoji in notifications. The one exception is an emoji that
is the thing that happened: "Rildy reacted 🩷 to your win."

## By surface

| Surface | The line | The slop it beats |
|---|---|---|
| Your own win (notification) | "Your best Sunday in three weeks." | "You logged 8 wins today!" |
| Crew (notification) | "Rildy just did the workout." / "Your crew had a day." | "Your friend completed an activity." |
| Looking back | "A year ago, you and your crew did this." / "This day last month." | "Memory from last year available." |
| Albums and recaps | "We made this for you." / "Your week, put together." The warmest the app gets, and still quiet. | |
| Empty state (in app only, never guilt) | "Nothing logged yet. Just the start of the day." | "No entries found." |
| A new idea, in context | One true thing, the first time they meet it. | "Long-press to resize to a medium block." |
| Error | "That didn't send. Try once more." | "Error: request failed (code 500)." |
| Quiet stretches | Concerned, never disappointed: "You've been quiet a few days. No rush." In the app only. | |

Streaks celebrate the habit and never scorekeep against people.

**In one sentence:** specific like a dashboard, warm like a friend who is
good with words, quiet like the calmest app on the phone.

## Rules that were already the app's, and still hold

`docs/ten.md` rule 4: one word per thing (Win, Block, Tower, Slot, Plan,
Crew, Memories), buttons in Title Case, never "missed" or "overdue",
nothing that reads as being watched. Where the Launch Pack's own examples
describe a gesture the app does not have ("Hold a win to make it bigger"),
the app's real gesture is what gets written.
