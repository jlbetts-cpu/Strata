# Launch and growth plan

Written 2026-10-08, overnight, from a research pass (sources inline; (a) data,
(b) what a real app did, (c) inference). The owner's goal, in his words: "we
have to plan for the app to go viral like I want it to. I think this is an
amazing product and im excited to show it to the world."

## The one idea

**Breakouts came from a dense friend cluster plus TikTok, not from features.**
Locket was #1 eleven days after launch off the founder's own TikTok and users
reposting it (b, appfigures.com/resources/insights/20220114). BeReal grew campus
by campus (b). Gas went state by state, tied to a school (b). Each new person's
first open needed people they already knew there (c). For Some Wins that is a
**crew**, and the thing people post is the **strip**.

## The loops this product actually has

1. **The strip is the shareable object.** Spotify reported 500M Wrapped shares
   in 24 hours because the share is designed in (a). The strip only travels if
   it leaves as a Story-sized (9:16) image with the brand on its back, and as a
   short video of it developing: the motion is the hook (c). Keep the mark
   small; Instagram demotes Reels with other apps' watermarks (a, 2021).
2. **Crews.** K = invites per user x conversion. Real programmes land at 0.2 to
   0.8 (a). If 30% start a crew, send 3 invites and 40% install, K = 0.36 (c):
   not viral alone, but every creator or paid install becomes about 1.5.
   Shorten invite to first win as much as raising K (a).
3. **Timing.** Ask for an invite after the first strip prints, the emotional
   peak; never in onboarding (c). Lapse forced 5 invites before use, hit #1,
   was called predatory and lost 70% of downloads in six weeks (b).

## Rules that bind growth (App Review)

- 5.1.1(v): inviting friends is never required to use the app.
- 5.1.2: Contacts only at the person's own initiative, no "select all".
- 3.2.2(x): no incentivised reviews or ratings, ever.
- Rewarding the INVITER with something cosmetic has passed review; never the
  invitee (b, developer forums 674832).
- A tip jar through in-app purchase is allowed (3.1.1).

**The trailer** is for the App Store page and the press kit. On feeds, cut it
into 7 to 12 second clips that open on the strip developing (c). TikTok wants
the point in the first three seconds (a).

**ADHD content norms.** Over half the top 100 #ADHD TikToks misstate symptoms
and about half the creators sell something (a). No symptom claims, no "do you
have ADHD?" hooks. "Made for brains that need wins", never treatment.

## Launch week

- Weeks -3 to 0: waitlist and pre-order, featuring nomination, seed crews.
- Day 0: App Store live, Product Hunt, press embargo, creator posts.
- Days 1 to 7: a TikTok a day; answer comments in video.
- Day 7: a numbers recap post.

## How we will know (targets)

Activation: 3 wins on day 0, or a printed strip within 48 hours. Retention to
beat: lifestyle averages D1 23%, D30 9% (a, Adjust); aim for D1 35%, D7 18%,
D30 10%. **Crew members should retain at about twice solo users**, or crews
are not the loop and the plan changes. Share rate = strips shared / printed.
