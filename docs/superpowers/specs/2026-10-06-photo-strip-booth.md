# The photo strip and its booth

2026-10-06. The owner: "the strip needs to feel like our app, it's like its main
representation, so time needs to be put into it." His reference: a dark booth
strip packed like a tower (wide frames across, small ones paired), the wordmark
and a signature at the foot, and the same strip blurred and dark before it
develops.

## Decisions (his)

- The header printer is gone (it "looks like an error"). Reaching the goal:
  the tower dances, then the **booth opens by itself** and prints.
- The strip **grows all day**. Before the goal it is shown undeveloped (dark,
  blurred) in Your day. The goal prints it; you **shake to develop** it.
- The foot: the wordmark, then **your name and the date** ("Jayden · Oct 6").
- **Up to 8 frames**; you choose which.
- Paper **white or black** only.
- **Doodled blocks go in** (the block's colour with its ink); **plain colour
  blocks never do**.
- **Stickers anywhere and doodles on it** are a must.
- **Crews get a strip** of their day too.
- **Its own screen**, the print a selling point, and the strip **turns in the
  hand** like a real one.

## The strip

Frames follow the tower's sizes, two columns wide:

| Win | Frame |
|---|---|
| Quick | half the width, square; paired with the next Quick |
| Regular | full width, wide (2:1) |
| Deep | full width, 4:3 |

A Quick with no partner takes the full width at 2:1. Order is the day's order.
The foot is the wordmark left-aligned with the signature under it.

## The booth

1. A glass printer across the top. The strip steps out of its slot a frame at
   a time, a tick and a light tap each, undeveloped.
2. It drops free and settles in the middle. "Shake to develop." Each shake
   lifts the dark and the blur a step; a tap does the same for anyone who
   cannot shake. Developed: a success tap, and the tools arrive.
3. Drag to turn it: it tilts and turns in 3D with a sheen across the paper,
   and turns all the way over to its back (the mark and the date, as a photo's
   back is stamped). It springs back to rest.
4. Tools: **Edit** (choose frames, paper, stickers, doodle), **Save**,
   **Share**, **Done**.

A day already developed opens straight to the strip.

## Out of scope for now

Strips for past days (Memories), and the widget.
