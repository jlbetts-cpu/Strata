# The crew chat: craft, what only we can do, and a build list (research, 2026-10-15)

The owner's ask: "creating a flawless chat experience, researching how other
apps do their chats and finding ways to implement the best additions while
also creating a unique experience. I want people to want to message on our
app over other apps."

Research only. No Swift was changed to write this. Read against
`~/Developer/Strata` on 2026-10-14.

**Tags.** (a) published docs or research: Apple's HIG, Apple developer docs,
App Review Guidelines, papers. (b) seen in a shipping app, with a source.
(c) my inference, or a claim about another app that rests on memory; marked
"unverified" where it matters.

**Not repeated here.** `docs/chat-plus-research-2026-10-13.md` (the plus
row) and `docs/qol-research-2026-10-13.md` sections 2 and 3 (M1 to M9: hold
menu, reply, heart, unsent, full screen, times, "clears at midnight", no jump
while reading, the drawer). Both are mostly built. This doc starts from
where they stop and cites them as [CP] and [QoL].

## What exists today (10 lines)

1. A one-day crew chat in a drawer on the tower (`ChatRestBar` at rest with the newest line, the +, field and Send; `ChatDrawer` rising to 62% or full).
2. Yours right in ink, friends' left on `quietFill`; face and name open a friend's run; runs are told apart by air; every bubble the same `radiusSurface`, no tails.
3. A centred time over the day's first line and after any hour gap (`ChatTimes`); "Today's chat clears at midnight." tops the thread and the empty state (`ChatClears`).
4. Bottom-anchored thread, interactive keyboard dismiss, no jump while reading plus a Newest button with the dot (`ChatFollow`).
5. Hold (0.35s) opens a popover: reaction row (+ for emoji, 🔥 👑 ❤️, a sticker at the end), then Reply, Copy, and Report (friend) or Delete with Undo (yours).
6. Double tap hearts a line with a burst; reactions sit as chips under the line, count past one, yours on a deeper fill.
7. Reply quotes a line ("↪ Sam: words") or a win; a tap on a quote scrolls to the line and lifts it, or opens the win.
8. The + row: Win (lands as a line with its photo), Plan (a card with I'm In), Sticker (doodle inside), Strip; a look-back card and today's plans pinned over the thread.
9. Sends go through an outbox and draw at once; after 10s unsent a line dims and says "That didn't send. Try once more."; drafts survive per crew; 280 characters, 200 lines a day, word filter.
10. No read receipts, no seen marks, no typing; Report and Block reachable from the hold, the face menu, the picture viewer and VoiceOver actions; chat alerts are local, one per crew per hour.

## 1. The craft details, against iMessage

For each: what iMessage does, what others do where it teaches something, what
our code does, and the verdict. "Has" means built; "Gap" means worth building;
"Keep" means ours is different on purpose and right.

### 1.1 Bubble grouping and tails

- **iMessage.** Consecutive bubbles from one sender stack tight; the tail is
  drawn only on the last bubble of the run, and the inner corners of the
  other bubbles are tightened so the run reads as one voice (b) [G1]
  (third-party reconstructions of the shape, not Apple's spec). In a group,
  the sender's name sits over the run and their picture sits beside it (b)
  [M7].
- **Ours.** Every bubble is the same rounded rectangle at `radiusSurface`
  (`CrewChatSheet.lineFace`). Within a run lines sit `GridConstants.spacing`
  apart; a new run gets `gapItem` (`row`). The face sits beside the run's
  first line, the name above it.
- **Verdict: Gap, small, no tails.** Tails are a Messages signature and a
  copy of it would read as a costume (c). The cheap part that does the work
  is the corner: on the sender's side, the first line keeps its top corner
  full and squares the bottom a little, middle lines square both, the last
  keeps the bottom full. One pure function (`first / middle / last / alone`)
  in `CrewChatLines.swift`, read in `lineFace`. (c)

### 1.2 Timestamps on demand

- **iMessage.** Drag the thread left and every bubble's time slides in;
  release and they hide (b) [M8]. A date and time header at gaps (b) [QoL A3].
- **Ours.** M5: a centred time at the day's start and after an hour's gap;
  VoiceOver reads every line's time. No per-line time for sighted users.
  [QoL] rejected swipe-left as a hidden gesture.
- **Verdict: Gap, tiny.** Put the line's time at the head of the hold menu,
  one quiet line over the reaction row ("4:12 PM"). The hold is already the
  one place you go to ask about a line; no new gesture, nothing on screen at
  rest. (c)

### 1.3 Scroll anchoring and the keyboard

- **iMessage.** Opens at the newest, the keyboard pushes the thread up with
  it, a drag down puts the keys away with the finger (b, c).
- **Ours.** `defaultScrollAnchor(.bottom)` and `.bottom, for: .sizeChanges`,
  `scrollDismissesKeyboard(.interactively)`, `snapToNewest` after the drawer
  and the keys land (`thread`, `onChange(of: writing)`).
- **Verdict: Has.** One risk worth watching (c): the newest-line snap waits a
  fixed 380ms for the keys. On a slow phone or with a third-party keyboard it
  can land early. If it is ever seen, drive it from the keyboard frame, not a
  timer.

### 1.4 The send moment

- **iMessage.** The field empties and the new bubble rises out of it into
  place, with a sound (b, c, unverified as to the exact motion). Hold Send
  for bubble effects: Slam, Loud, Gentle, Invisible Ink (b) [M4]. iOS 18
  added per-word text effects (b) [M4]. Telegram: hold Send for message
  effects (b) [T1].
- **Ours.** `send()` empties the field, the outbox draws the line, the thread
  scrolls on `motionSnappy`. **The row has no transition, and there is no
  haptic on send.** The line simply appears.
- **Verdict: Gap, small, and the highest felt-quality per line of code.**
  Your own newest line comes up from the field's height and settles on the
  app's `elasticPop` (a move from the bottom combined with opacity; a cross
  fade under Reduce Motion), and `HapticsEngine.lightTap()` on Send. Friends'
  lines keep arriving as they do. (c) Effects behind a held Send are in
  section 2 as an owner's call.

### 1.5 Haptics

- **HIG** (a) [H1], paraphrased: use haptics to confirm meaningful results,
  keep each one meaning the same thing everywhere, do not overuse them, and
  pair them with something seen.
- **Ours.** `lightTap` on hold, double tap, reply, quote taps; `tick` on
  Copy; `warning` once when a line first fails; `success` on a strip sent.
  Nothing on Send (see 1.4) and, rightly, nothing when a friend's line
  arrives while you are reading (c).
- **Verdict: Gap, one.** Send. Nothing else.

### 1.6 Tapback placement, and who reacted

- **iMessage.** Tapbacks sit on the bubble's top corner, overlapping it.
  iOS 18: any emoji or sticker, coloured classic six, and a tap on a received
  Tapback shows who sent what (b) [M2]. Stickers on a bubble since 17.2 (b)
  [M1].
- **Ours.** Chips under the line on its side (`ChatReactionChips`). **Not a
  button**: who chose what is only in the VoiceOver label. Sighted people in
  a crew of eight cannot see who gave the 🔥.
- **Verdict: Keep the placement, Gap on who.** Under the line is more legible
  for stickers at 22pt than a corner badge, and it is already the win's
  pattern. A tap on the chips opens the faces with their marks, the
  `ReactorRow` the win viewer already has (`CrewReactions.swift`, "Everyone
  who reacted, as Messages shows it"). (b) [M2], (c)

### 1.7 Reply previews

- **iMessage.** Swipe a bubble right: a focused reply view over a blurred
  thread; replies draw as a thread with a connecting line (b) [M5].
  **WhatsApp**: swipe right, the quote sits over the field with an x; the
  sent bubble carries the quoted message inside it (b) [W1].
- **Ours.** "↪ Sam: words" over the field with an x (`replyQuote`); the sent
  bubble carries the quote as one smaller line at its top (`lineFace`).
- **Verdict: Has.** One small polish (c): inside the bubble the quote is set
  off only by size and tone. A quiet inset (the bubble's own fill one step
  deeper behind the quote, no bar, no border) would make "this is someone
  else's words" readable at a glance. Optional.

### 1.8 Jump to the quoted message

- **Telegram.** Tapping a quote scrolls to the exact spot with an animation
  and highlights it (b) [T2].
- **Ours.** `goTo`: scrolls the line to centre and lifts it to 1.04 for
  700ms. A tap on a win's quote opens the win.
- **Verdict: Has.** Matches the best of the set.

### 1.9 Unread separator

- **iMessage.** A catch-up arrow at the top right jumps to the first message
  you have not seen (b) [M1]. **Discord** draws a "New" divider at the first
  unread and offers Mark As Read (b) [D1].
- **Ours.** The dot on the rest bar; opening marks everything seen at once
  (`SocialStore.markChatSeen`); Newest handles lines arriving while open.
  **Nothing marks where you left off.** Open the chat after a busy afternoon
  and you land at the newest with no way to know which lines are new.
- **Verdict: Gap, medium.** One quiet word, "New", centred in `inkTertiary`
  above the first line that arrived since you last opened (no rule line, the
  chat's own way). If it is more than a screen up, the chat opens on it
  instead of the foot. It goes when the chat closes. **Local only**: the
  seen set is already on this phone (`chatSeen`), so nobody learns anything.
  The catch: `markChatSeen` runs on open, so the chat must take its
  snapshot of the old seen set before marking. (c)

### 1.10 Swipe to reply

- **iMessage** since iOS 17 (b) [M5]; **WhatsApp** for years (b) [W1].
  Instagram replies by hold (b) [I1].
- **Ours.** Reply is in the hold menu only. [QoL] M2c chose that because
  `docs/ten.md` rule 2 gives a row's swipe to removing it and the drawer owns
  vertical drags.
- **Verdict: Owner's call.** A horizontal drag on a bubble does not collide
  with the drawer's vertical one, and the chat's bubbles are not removable
  rows, so rule 2's reason does not quite reach them (c). It is the gesture
  every friend group already has in its thumbs (b). Recommended: yes, a short
  rightward pull that reveals the reply arrow, one `tick` at the threshold,
  release to reply. Listed as a question, not in the build list.

### 1.11 The long-press menu

- **iMessage** (b, exact rows unverified): the bubble lifts, the thread
  blurs, the Tapback row sits above, then Reply, Copy, and on your own Undo
  Send and Edit, then More. iOS 18 adds the emoji picker and stickers to the
  Tapback row (b) [M2].
- **Ours.** A popover hung off the bubble (`blockMenu`): reaction row, Reply,
  Copy where there are words, Report or Delete. Heart was removed from the
  rows as clutter (owner, 2026-10-14).
- **Verdict: Has, plus 1.2's time line.** No lift and blur: that is a system
  context menu's look and the app chose its own menu drawing. Do not add Edit
  ([QoL] "Different on purpose").

### 1.12 Links

- **iMessage** turns a link into a rich preview card (b, c).
- **Ours.** `Text(message.text)`, no detection anywhere in the chat (no
  `NSDataDetector` or link attribute in `Strata/`). [QoL] SHOULD: links that
  open, no preview card. Not built.
- **Verdict: Gap, small.** Detect web addresses, underline them in the
  line's own ink, open in Safari. No preview: a preview is a request to
  someone else's server on a friend's behalf ([QoL]). (c)

### 1.13 Photos

- **iMessage.** Inline, tap for full screen, a grey frame at the final shape
  while loading (b, c).
- **Ours.** Tap opens `ChatPictureViewer` (M4, built). **While loading,
  `ChatPicture` draws `Color.clear` at 160 by 160 and the image then lands at
  up to 160 by 213; `CrewStripPicture` draws clear at 120 by 360.** The
  thread shifts when a picture lands, and a clear frame reads as a missing
  line.
- **Verdict: Gap, small.** `quietFill` at the final aspect while loading
  ([QoL] SHOULD, H7 "Show something as soon as possible"). The aspect needs
  to be known before the image is read: square for stickers, 3:4 for a win
  photo, the strip's own ratio.

### 1.14 Empty state

- **iMessage** shows an empty thread (b). **Ours**: "Quiet here. Yet." and
  the clears line.
- **Verdict: Opportunity, see section 2.4.** An empty chat on a crew that has
  wins today has something to say: the newest win is right there.

### 1.15 Sent and delivered, without the pressure

- **iMessage** shows Delivered, and Read where turned on, under your last
  message in a one-to-one; group chats have no read receipts (b, community
  sources only) [M9].
- **Ours.** Nothing on success; after 10s, "That didn't send." with Try
  Again (M1).
- **Verdict: Keep.** Success is silence; only failure speaks. This is
  already the right answer for a crew app, and "Sent" under every line would
  invite the "Read" question (c). See section 3.2.

### 1.16 Emoji alone

- **iMessage** draws one to three emoji with no other text larger and without
  a bubble (b, unverified as to the exact count).
- **Ours.** Drawn at body size in a bubble. [QoL] SHOULD, not built.
- **Verdict: Gap, tiny.** A pure check in `CrewChatLines.swift`, twice body
  size, no fill, as a sticker sits.

### 1.17 Notifications that look like a friend

- **iMessage** notifications carry the sender's picture, and any messaging
  app may do the same through communication notifications: an
  `INSendMessageIntent` donated with the sender as an `INPerson` and their
  image, plus the Communication Notifications capability (a) [A1] (page
  confirmed; the API names are from memory, verify before building). Inline
  reply from the notification is a `UNTextInputNotificationAction` (a, c).
- **Ours.** Chat alerts are the app's own, at most one a crew an hour
  (`CrewNotifications.chatNews`, `ChatAlerts`); the crew is the title; no
  sender picture, no reply from the alert. `SomeWinsNotifications/
  NotificationService.swift` already rewrites pings for wins.
- **Verdict: Gap, large, and the most "want to message here" item outside the
  app.** The friend's **head** as the notification's face is the app's
  identity on the lock screen, and Reply from the alert brings a line in
  without opening anything (c). The hourly cap stays.

## 2. What only this chat can do

Every other chat on the list is about the conversation. This one sits on a
tower of the day's wins, lasts one day, and every person in it has a head.
That is the whole advantage: **a message here can be about something that
happened, and can be answered with something you do** (c).

The references, briefly:

- **BeReal RealMojis**: you react with a photo of your own face making the
  expression; six saved ones plus an Instant one taken live; one per post (b)
  [B1].
- **Locket**: you answer a friend's photo with a photo of your own; a
  reaction tells them you saw it and the app says it does not count
  reactions (b) [L1].
- **Retro**: one moment a week to a private circle, no public likes; the
  talk happens in private messages (b) [R1].
- **Snapchat**: chats clear by default, a hold saves a line for both people,
  and the Bitmoji in the corner says a friend is in the chat right now (b)
  [S1]. The last is the presence this app refuses.
- **Geneva**: rooms of five kinds with polls, pins, threads, events (b) [Ge1].
  The lesson is the opposite: everything a big group needs is clutter for
  eight friends (c).
- **Instagram DMs**: hold for a suggested emoji bar, edit within 15 minutes,
  pin three chats, nicknames per chat (b) [I1].

## 3. Constraints

### 3.1 CloudKit latency, and typing indicators

What the chat can know about other phones (from the code):
`CrewLivePace` asks every 3s while things move, 12s when idle, and once
pushes are seen to arrive, every 8s or 40s with the push doing the telling.
A CloudKit subscription notifies every device except the one that made the
change, and the push is a signal to fetch, not the data (a) [A2]. Pushes are
not guaranteed; developers report configured subscriptions that never
deliver (b) [A3]. So a line reaches a friend in about 1 to 3 seconds on a
good day and up to 40 on a bad one (c, from the intervals).

Typing indicators, the options:

1. **None** (today, and [QoL]'s decision). Free. Fits the app.
2. **An ephemeral "writing" record** per person in the crew's zone, written
   on the first keystroke and expiring after ~10s. Each write is a CloudKit
   save and a push fanned to up to seven phones; it arrives 1 to 8 seconds
   late and lingers after the line lands, so it is wrong most of the time it
   shows (c). It also counts against the starter's iCloud the way
   `CrewLivePace` was cut back to avoid.
3. **Typing carried on the push of the line itself.** Nothing to show:
   by then the line has arrived.

**Recommended: none.** iOS 26 added group typing to Messages (b) [M3], so its
absence will be noticed by a few; the answer is speed of the line itself, not
a dot. What would help instead (c): on send, `liveNudge` the crew's pace back
to quick on this phone (it does), and make sure the line's own push is what
wakes friends' open chats, which the chase logic in `CrewLivePace` covers.

One ordering note (c): lines sort by the sender's `createdAt`. A friend whose
clock runs a minute behind can land a line above ones you already read. Rare,
and the "New" marker of 1.9 should key on what was seen, not on time, so it
still lands right.

### 3.2 Privacy: no read receipts pressure

Read receipts and "seen" are linked to anxiety and more checking; bad
feelings come sooner when a message is read and not answered (a) [QoL R1, R2,
R3]. Instagram added a switch to turn them off (b) [I1]. Snapchat's presence
Bitmoji is the extreme (b) [S1].

Rules for everything above (c):
- Nothing tells a person who opened, read, reached or tapped anything. The
  "New" marker (1.9) is local. Me Too (2.3) tells nobody. Who reacted (1.6)
  shows only what people chose to send.
- Success is silent (1.15). Failure speaks once.
- No "last active", no "in the chat now", no typing.

### 3.3 Moderation: Report and Block stay one hold away

App Review 1.2 asks of any app where people post to each other: a filter for
objectionable material, a way to report it with timely responses, a way to
block abusive users, and published contact information (a) [AR1].

Where they are today: Report on a friend's line's hold, Block and Report on
their face, Report in the picture viewer, both as VoiceOver actions, the word
filter on every line (`CrewWords` in `messages(in:)` and on send), photos
through the on-device check (`photoIsShown`), the 200-a-day cap.

For each new thing above:
- **Win-line cheers (2.1)**: a reaction, as today; nothing new to report.
- **Head stickers (2.2)**: a picture. It must go through the same sticker
  path so a held reaction or line still offers Report, and a head made from
  a photo has already passed the head's own checks (c, verify).
- **Me Too (2.3)**: sends nothing.
- **Links (1.12)**: words, through `CrewWords`. A link to anything is still a
  line someone can Report (c). No preview, so nothing is fetched.
- **Cheer send (2.6)**: a marked line; its words go through the filter.
- **Notification reply (1.17)**: the reply must go through `SocialStore.send`
  so the filter, the throttle and the daily cap all apply; a refused reply
  must say so the next time the app opens (c).
