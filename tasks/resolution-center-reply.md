# Reply to the 4.1(a) rejection

Paste into App Store Connect, the app, TestFlight, the rejected build, **Resolution
Center**, Reply.

**Why it asks a question instead of arguing.** The notice is Apple's boilerplate
for the whole of 4.1(a) and names three unrelated example causes. Two submissions
have now been rejected with the identical text, so another change made on a guess
costs another cycle and still does not say what the reviewer saw. The reply below
states what the app is, removes the candidates that can be ruled out on fact, and
asks them to name the element.

---

Hello,

Thank you for the review. I want to resolve this properly, but I cannot tell
which element created the association, and I would rather fix the right thing
than guess.

Some facts that may narrow it down:

This submission is for TestFlight external beta review. The App Store listing
has no keywords, no description, no promotional text and no screenshots. The
only metadata present is the app name, the subtitle, the icon, and the beta
test description.

The app is called Strata Neo. The name refers to strata, the layers in rock,
because the app stacks each finished task as a block into a growing tower. It
is not derived from and does not reference any other app.

The icon is an original mark, an abstract letter S drawn as two offset bars. It
was made for this app and is not adapted from any other source.

The app contains no third-party content. There are no imported or licensed
assets, no characters, and no references to other apps, brands or services. The
photographs in the app are my own. There is no account, no server, and no
network request of any kind.

The app itself is a private record of things a person has finished, with an
optional camera and a map of where the photographs were taken. Everything stays
on the device.

Could you tell me which specific element created the misleading association:
the app name, the subtitle, the icon, the beta test description, or something
in the app's own interface? I will change it straight away.

Thank you,
Jayden Betts

---

## While that is open, the beta is not actually blocked

Beta App Review gates the **external** group only. Build 34 is VALID and already
in the internal group "bugs", and internal TestFlight has no review at all.

Internal testing takes up to 100 people. Anyone added as a user on the App Store
Connect account, at any role including Developer, can be made an internal tester
and installs immediately. Users and Access, invite by email, then TestFlight,
the internal group, add them.

So friends can be on build 34 today. The external public link is the only thing
waiting on 4.1(a).
